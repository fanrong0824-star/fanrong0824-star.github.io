About

Rong Fan (樊荣)

I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the State Key Laboratory of Resources and Environmental Information System (LREIS), Institute of Geographic Sciences and Natural Resources Research (IGSNRR), Chinese Academy of Sciences, and a Visiting Scholar at the Earth System Modelling group, Technical University of Munich (TUM).

My research combines physical oceanography, Earth-system modelling, and physics-informed artificial intelligence. I study the global mid-depth ocean circulation using ocean observations and numerical models, and investigate differentiable modelling and early warning signals for climate-system tipping dynamics.

I received my Ph.D. in Geographic Information Science from IGSNRR, Chinese Academy of Sciences (2025).

Research

My research interests span ocean dynamics, machine learning for Earth-system modelling, and nonlinear climate dynamics.

Physics-informed artificial intelligence for Earth-system modelling. I develop and apply machine learning approaches constrained by physical principles to improve the representation and prediction of ocean circulation and Earth-system processes.

Mid-depth ocean circulation modelling and change analysis. I investigate the circulation of the ocean interior by comparing global ocean-model simulations with in-situ observations, assessing model biases and studying large-scale patterns of mid-depth flow and connectivity.

Differentiable modelling and climate tipping dynamics. I am interested in differentiable Earth-system models and early warning signals of abrupt climate change, including ongoing work on potential tipping of the Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC).
