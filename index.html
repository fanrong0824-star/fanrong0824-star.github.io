Research

I am broadly interested in the fluid dynamics of the ocean and its role in the Earth system. My current research directions include:

Mesoscale and submesoscale dynamics. Eddies, fronts, instabilities, and the pathways by which energy and tracers move between spatial scales.

Ocean circulation. The physical mechanisms regulating large-scale transport, overturning, and the connections between local dynamics and basin-scale circulation.

Air–sea interaction. How atmospheric forcing, upper-ocean responses, and coupled variability regulate exchanges of heat, momentum, and freshwater.

Waves and mixing. The influence of internal waves, stratification, and turbulent mixing on ocean structure and transport.
